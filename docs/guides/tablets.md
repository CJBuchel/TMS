# Setting up tablets

Any tablet with a current browser works. iPads are the usual choice.

## Each tablet

1. Join the event Wi-Fi.
2. Scan the launcher's QR code, or type the address.
3. Scan the station's key card with the camera, or choose **Access key** and type the key (Event setup → Access keys). The tablet remembers its table or pod and goes straight to its station.
4. In Safari, use **Share → Add to Home Screen**, so TMS opens full screen without the address bar.
5. Turn on **Guided Access** (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access). Triple-click the side button inside TMS to start it, so the tablet stays in TMS.
6. Set **Auto-Lock** to Never, or to a long time, and keep the tablet charged.

## When things go wrong

- **No connection:** carry on. Scoresheets and rubrics are saved on the tablet and sent when the connection is back. The station shows how many are waiting.
- **A sheet needs a team or round:** open Settings on the tablet. Every sheet made there is listed; choose the team and round and send it again.
- **The wrong table:** log out and log in with the right key.
- **A lost tablet:** revoke its key (Event setup → Access keys). That tablet is logged out at once, and a new key can go on a spare.
